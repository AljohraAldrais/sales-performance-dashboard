# Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide a clear view of sales performance, profitability, costs, customers, products, and order status.

## Dashboard Preview

![Sales Performance Dashboard](Screenshots/Sales_Performance_Dashboard.png)

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data and presenting key business metrics through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard helps users monitor overall sales performance, compare regional results, analyze product and customer performance, and track costs and order status.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
- Compare sales and profit across regions.
- Analyze monthly sales and cost trends.
- Identify top-performing products and customers.
- Monitor order status distribution.
- Compare sales and cost across product categories.
- Track customer and order activity over time.

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Cost
- Total Quantity
- Sales Target Achievement

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive filtering by Region, Order Status, Product, Customer, and Date.
- Monthly sales performance analysis.
- Regional sales and profit comparison.
- Order status distribution.
- Top 5 products by sales.
- Top 5 customers by sales.
- Monthly cost analysis.
- Sales and cost comparison by category.
- Monthly customer and order analysis.

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization
